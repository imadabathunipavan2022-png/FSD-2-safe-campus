💳 Lost Card Support
⚠️ If your college ID card or access card is lost, report it as soon as possible so the card can be blocked and unauthorized use can be prevented.

🆘 Lost Card Actions

🏢 Contact Campus Office 🛡️ Campus Security 📝 Report Lost Card 💳 Card Office 📍 Find Campus Security 🔎 Lost & Found

📚 What To Do If Your Card Is Lost

1️⃣ Report Immediately

Inform the appropriate college office or authorized campus official as soon as you notice that your card is missing.

2️⃣ Block The Card

Ask the authorized card administrator to deactivate the lost card to reduce the risk of unauthorized access.

3️⃣ Check Lost & Found

Contact the campus lost-and-found service and provide the relevant details needed to identify your card.

4️⃣ Request Replacement

Follow your institution's procedure for obtaining a replacement student ID or access card.

🔐 Protect Your Information

Do not share sensitive personal information publicly while reporting a lost card. Use official college channels whenever possible.

✅ Keep The New Card Safe

After receiving a replacement card, keep it secure and follow the campus rules for using and protecting your student identification.
