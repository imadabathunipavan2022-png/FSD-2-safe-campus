📚 Know Your Rights & Safety

🚫 Ragging Is Not Acceptable

Students should be able to participate in college activities without intimidation, humiliation, threats, or unwanted treatment.

📢 Report Early

If something feels unsafe, inform an authorized campus official, counsellor, or other trusted support person as soon as possible.

🤝 Support Others

If you witness inappropriate behaviour, encourage the affected student to seek help and inform the appropriate campus authority.

🔒 Privacy

Use the reporting channels provided by your institution and ask the responsible authority how reports and personal information are handled.

🧑‍🏫 Counselling Support

Students can approach an authorized counsellor or student-support service when they need help dealing with a difficult campus situation.

🛡️ Stay Safe

Move toward a safe and supervised location when you feel threatened and seek assistance from responsible campus personnel.

Your safety and dignity matter.
