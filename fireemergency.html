🔥 College Fire Emergency
⚠️ In case of fire, stay calm, alert others, evacuate immediately and follow authorized emergency procedures.

🔥 Fire Emergency Services

🚒 Call Fire Engine (101) 👨‍💼 Inform Campus Officials 🚪 Nearby Fire Exits 🆘 Fire Help Centre 📍 Emergency Assembly Point 📝 Report Fire Incident

🧯 Fire Safety Information

🔥 Fire Safety Instructions

Stay calm and immediately alert people nearby. Do not use elevators during a fire. Follow the nearest safe emergency exit and move to a safe location.

🧯 Fire Extinguisher Guide

Use a fire extinguisher only if the fire is small and you have been trained to use it. Keep a safe distance and never put yourself at risk.

🚪 Evacuation Procedure

Leave the building using the nearest safe exit. Do not run or push others. Follow instructions from campus officials and emergency personnel.

💨 Smoke Safety

If there is heavy smoke, stay low and move toward a safe exit. Avoid breathing smoke and follow emergency evacuation instructions.

📍 Assembly Point

After leaving the building, move to the designated emergency assembly point. Stay there until campus officials confirm that it is safe to return.

📞 Emergency Numbers

Fire Emergency: 101
General Emergency: 112
Campus Emergency: Contact your campus officials.

Stay calm. Stay safe. Follow emergency instructions.
