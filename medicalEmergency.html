Health Resources

💊 First Aid Guide
First aid knowledge empowers students to respond quickly to injuries such as cuts, burns, sprains, or fainting. Knowing how to clean wounds, apply bandages, and perform CPR can help until professional help arrives.
🧠 Mental Health Support
College life can be stressful. Mental health support includes counseling services, stress management workshops, and peer support groups. Students are encouraged to seek help early to maintain emotional well-being.
💉 Vaccination Information
Vaccinations help protect against contagious diseases. The medical center can provide vaccination schedules, reminders, and health awareness information.
🍎 Healthy Lifestyle Tips
A healthy lifestyle includes balanced nutrition, regular exercise, adequate sleep, and avoiding harmful habits. Small healthy habits can improve overall physical and mental well-being.
Stay safe. Your health is our priority.
