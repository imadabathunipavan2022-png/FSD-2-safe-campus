🔐 Campus Safety Information

🛡️ Campus Security

Campus security personnel help maintain a safe environment and can guide students toward appropriate support services.

👀 Stay Aware

Stay aware of your surroundings and follow campus safety instructions, especially in unfamiliar or crowded areas.

📢 Report Concerns

Report suspicious or unsafe situations through the official campus reporting channels so the responsible authorities can respond.

🚪 Emergency Exits

Become familiar with emergency exits and designated safe areas in the buildings you regularly use.

📍 Safe Locations

Identify supervised areas such as campus security offices, administrative offices, reception areas, or other designated safe locations.

🤝 Get Support

If you are concerned about your safety, seek help from an authorized campus official, counsellor, security officer, or trusted adult.

Stay alert. Stay safe. Help keep your campus secure.
